Simulator — Nations League–style format

DirectThe Top 8 from qualifiers skip the groups and go straight to the Round of 16.
GroupsThe other 24 play 3 groups of 8 over 4 matchdays. One BO1 per matchday, inside your group.
MovementWin and you move up a group. Lose and you move down.
PointsWin: 2 pts in Group 1, 1 pt in Groups 2 and 3. Lose by under 30s: bonus of +1 (Group 1) or +0.5 (Groups 2 and 3).
QualifyAfter matchday 4 the 8 best by points join the Round of 16. Tiebreak: average of your 2 best races.
